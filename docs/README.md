# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Scrum Master / Delivery Facilitator

### Role Summary
The Scrum Master or Delivery Facilitator enables the delivery team to work effectively, promotes agreed delivery practices, and helps remove team-level impediments.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove or escalate team-level blockers and improve team flow
- Reinforce the Definition of Done and delivery agreements
- Help the team manage dependencies and capacity

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers on sequencing, plans, cross-team dependencies, and escalations
- Partners with Developers and QA/Testing to identify flow, quality, and process issues
- Supports Product Managers by keeping backlog discussions and reviews focused on decisions

---

## Tech Lead / Engineering Lead

### Role Summary
The Tech Lead or Engineering Lead provides technical direction and ensures implementation decisions are maintainable, testable, and aligned with project goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical approach and architecture boundaries
- Lead design and code reviews
- Identify technical risks and recommend mitigations
- Guide estimation, testability, and implementation quality

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers on design, implementation, mentoring, and engineering standards
- Advises Project Managers on technical risks, dependencies, and delivery estimates
- Collaborates with Product Managers on feasibility and technical trade-offs
- Coordinates with QA/Testing on test strategies and automation

---

## Release Manager / Release Coordinator

### Role Summary
The Release Manager or Release Coordinator owns release readiness and coordinates deployment activities across delivery, quality, security, operations, and stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain release plans and readiness checklists
- Coordinate approvals, deployment windows, smoke tests, and announcements
- Confirm rollback and incident mitigation plans are available
- Verify post-deployment checks and communicate release status

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Coordinates Developers and QA/Testing on merged changes, acceptance, and verification
- Works with Project Managers on schedules, communications, and risk decisions
- Partners with Security and Operations / SRE Leads on controls, observability, and rollback readiness
- Provides Product Managers and stakeholders with release scope and status

---

## UX Designer / User Researcher

### Role Summary
The UX Designer or User Researcher represents customer needs in product decisions through research, interaction design, and usability validation.

### Responsibilities
- Research customer problems and usability risks
- Create and validate workflows, prototypes, and interface specifications
- Define experience and accessibility expectations
- Incorporate feedback and usage evidence into design decisions

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers on problem framing, outcomes, and prioritization
- Works with Developers on feasibility, implementation details, and design fidelity
- Collaborates with QA/Testing on usability, accessibility, and acceptance criteria
- Shares findings with Project Managers and stakeholders during planning and reviews

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
The Security Lead defines security expectations, supports risk assessment, and ensures security controls are integrated into delivery and release.

### Responsibilities
- Identify threats and security requirements
- Review security-sensitive designs and mitigations
- Guide CI security checks, remediation, and incident practices
- Support evidence collection for release approval

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Advises Developers and Tech Leads on secure implementation
- Coordinates with QA/Testing on security test coverage
- Works with the Release Manager on release readiness and rollback decisions
- Escalates critical findings through Project Managers and the appropriate security incident process

---

## Data / Analytics Lead

### Role Summary
The Data or Analytics Lead establishes how project outcomes are measured and ensures teams can collect, analyze, and act on reliable evidence.

### Responsibilities
- Define measurement plans and analytical approaches
- Specify success metrics and instrumentation requirements
- Build or validate dashboards and experiment analysis
- Communicate evidence and uncertainty to decision-makers

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers to define success measures and assess outcomes
- Works with Developers to implement event tracking and reliable data collection
- Supports QA/Testing in validating instrumentation and analytics acceptance criteria
- Helps Project Managers report progress and outcome-based risks

---

## Operations / SRE Lead

### Role Summary
The Operations or Site Reliability Engineering Lead owns operational readiness, service reliability, observability, and production incident practices.

### Responsibilities
- Define service-level objectives and operational requirements
- Establish monitoring, alerting, runbooks, and on-call support
- Assess production readiness and capacity
- Coordinate incident response and post-incident improvements

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Developers and Tech Leads on reliability, scalability, and observability
- Partners with the Release Manager on deployment and rollback readiness
- Coordinates with Security on operational risks and incident response
- Supports Project Managers with production risks, dependencies, and escalations

---

## Customer Support / Solutions Lead

### Role Summary
The Customer Support or Solutions Lead brings frontline customer experience and production feedback into prioritization, delivery, and release communication.

### Responsibilities
- Consolidate customer issues, themes, and adoption feedback
- Define support readiness and customer communication needs
- Help triage production-impacting customer concerns
- Capture lessons that inform future roadmap and process improvements

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with Product Managers on problem statements and backlog prioritization
- Provides Developers and QA/Testing with reproducible examples and acceptance context
- Works with Project Managers on customer-impact escalations and stakeholder updates
- Coordinates with the Release Manager on release notes, support enablement, and known issues
